# Privacy Policy for Carwash Sale Tracker

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026  
**App Name:** Carwash Sale Tracker  
**Contact Email:** custoworks1@gmail.com  

---

## 1. Information We Collect
Carwash Sale Tracker collects minimal data required to provide carwash operations and sales management services:
- **Account Data:** Full Name and Email Address entered during registration (stored securely via Google Firebase).
- **Camera & Vehicle Photos:** Camera access is used solely for taking photos of vehicle license plates and payment receipts.
- **License Plate Recognition (OCR):** Photos are processed via Google ML Kit to extract license plate text.
- **Sales Transactions:** Record of packages, vehicle sizes, teams, amounts, and commissions stored in Cloud Firestore.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services
We use trusted Google and payment services:
- **Google Firebase:** User authentication and database storage.
- **Google AdMob:** Displaying non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements.
- **Google ML Kit:** License plate text recognition.
- **PayMongo:** Processing subscription payments via GCash, Maya, and credit cards.

---

## 3. Data Security
All user data is isolated per account using multi-tenant security rules. Account A cannot access or view Account B's records.

---

## 4. User Rights & Data Deletion
Users can request account or sales data deletion by emailing **custoworks1@gmail.com**.

## 5. Contact
