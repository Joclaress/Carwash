1. Information We Collect

Carwash Sale Tracker collects minimal data required to provide carwash business management services:

  • Account Information: Full name and email address provided during registration (authenticated via Google Firebase).
  • Vehicle Photos & Camera Usage: Camera access is used solely for taking photos of vehicle license plates and payment receipts. Photos are stored securely in Firebase Storage.
  • License Plate Recognition (OCR): Processed locally or via Google ML Kit to extract license plate numbers.
  • Sales & Financial Records: Transaction details, package prices, and commission breakdowns stored securely in Cloud Firestore.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes trusted third-party services that may process information under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication and database storage.
  • Google AdMob: Used for serving non-intrusive advertisements.
  • Google ML Kit: Used for on-device license plate text recognition.
  • PayMongo: Used for processing subscription payments securely via GCash, Maya, and credit cards.

3. Data Security & Storage

All user records are isolated on a multi-tenant basis. Each account can only access their own sales and business data. We enforce strict Firebase Security Rules to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated sales data at any time. To request data deletion, please contact us at custoworks1@gmail.com.

5. Children's Privacy

Carwash Sale Tracker does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
